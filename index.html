FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a standard event include?

Every standard package includes up to 2 hours of fully hosted gameplay, a complete mobile Nerf battle arena, a wide selection of blasters, safety eyewear for every participant, a high-volume dart supply, team battle equipment like flags and props, battle barriers, and a dedicated Nerf Battle Referee running all the games.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is based on group size. As your group grows, we expand the arena, add equipment, and adjust staffing so every player stays fully engaged. Events start at $595.00 for 1–15 participants (minimum booking) and scale up from there.

What if we have more than 35 participants?

Groups of 36 or more require special pricing. Give us a call at 786-671-NERF (6373) and we’ll build a package for your group.

Where can you set up the arena?

Almost anywhere—inside or outside, at your home, a park, a playground, a parking lot, or an office. We transform the space into a fully enclosed, battle-ready Nerf War zone.

Can we customize our event?

Absolutely. Choose from upgrades like Extended Ammo, Tactical or Mega blasters, Themed Battles, Night Ops, Water-Blaster battles, a Shooting Gallery add-on, and more—plus catering through our partnership with Domino’s Pizza.

Do you offer a Spanish-language site?

Yes! Visit our Spanish site for information en Español.
